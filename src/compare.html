02 / 怎么选How to choose

四个问题，
基本就能定方向。 Four questions
usually settle it.

资料都在飞书 / 钉钉里吗？Is everything already in Lark / DingTalk?

如果是，先把平台自带的知识问答用起来，成本最低。资料散落在共享盘、图纸系统、邮箱、ERP 里，才需要中间件。

If yes, start with the built-in Q&A — it's the cheapest. You need middleware when data is spread across file shares, drawing systems, email and ERP.

答错的代价有多大？How costly is a wrong answer?

工艺、质量、报价场景答错代价高，必须有段落级出处和拒答机制；内部闲聊式问答要求可以低一些。

In process, quality and quoting, wrong answers are expensive — you need paragraph-level citations and refusal. Casual internal Q&A can tolerate less.

数据能不能上云？Can the data go to the cloud?

图纸、配方、客户档案不能出网，就只能选可私有化、可断网的路线。

If drawings, formulas or customer records can't leave your network, only on-prem, air-gappable routes qualify.

有没有人长期维护？Who maintains it long-term?

有 AI 工程团队可以考虑开源平台或自研；没有的话，需要一个能陪跑到上线、再把运维交接给 IT 的伙伴。

With an AI engineering team, open-source or in-house works. Without one, you need a partner who runs it to go-live and then hands operations to IT.
